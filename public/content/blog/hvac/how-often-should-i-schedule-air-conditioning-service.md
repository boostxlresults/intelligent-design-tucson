# How Often Should I Schedule Air Conditioning Service?

Regular AC service is essential for keeping your system running efficiently in Tucson's demanding climate. But how often do you really need professional service? Here's a comprehensive guide to AC maintenance scheduling.

## Professional Service: Twice Per Year

### Spring Tune-Up (Essential)

Schedule cooling system service in March or April, before the intense heat arrives.

**Why spring timing matters:**
- Catches problems before you need cooling
- Avoids peak-season scheduling delays
- Ensures system is ready for heavy summer use
- Allows time for repairs if needed

### Fall Check-Up (Recommended)

If you have a heat pump or use your system for heating, schedule a fall tune-up in October or November.

**Fall service includes:**
- Heating system inspection
- Reversing valve check (heat pumps)
- Thermostat calibration
- Safety control testing

## Filter Maintenance: Monthly During Peak Season

Filters are the most important homeowner maintenance task.

### Recommended Schedule:
- **Summer (May-September)**: Check and change monthly
- **Shoulder seasons (March-April, October-November)**: Check monthly, change as needed
- **Winter (December-February)**: Check every 2-3 months

### When to Change More Often:
- Multiple pets in the home
- Family members with allergies or asthma
- Nearby construction or dusty conditions
- Home near busy roads

## Signs You Need Service Sooner

Don't wait for scheduled maintenance if you notice:

### Performance Issues:
- Reduced cooling capacity
- Uneven temperatures between rooms
- System running constantly
- Frequent cycling on and off
- Higher than normal energy bills

### Warning Sounds:
- Grinding or scraping
- Buzzing or humming
- Clicking that doesn't stop
- Banging or clanking

### Visual Signs:
- Ice on refrigerant lines or coils
- Water pooling around indoor unit
- Visible damage to outdoor unit
- Dirty or clogged coils

### Odors:
- Musty or moldy smell
- Burning smell
- Chemical or refrigerant odor

## Service Schedule by System Age

### New Systems (0-5 years)
- Annual professional tune-up (spring)
- Monthly filter changes during cooling season
- Minimal additional service typically needed
- Keep all warranty documentation

### Mid-Life Systems (5-10 years)
- Annual professional tune-up (spring)
- Consider fall tune-up as well
- Monthly filter maintenance
- Watch for developing issues
- May need occasional repairs

### Older Systems (10+ years)
- Twice-yearly professional service
- Monthly filter changes
- More frequent monitoring
- Consider replacement planning
- Expect more frequent repairs

## Arizona-Specific Considerations

Tucson's climate creates unique service needs:

### Pre-Summer Check (Essential)
- System runs 6-8 months per year
- Extreme heat stresses all components
- Failure during summer is dangerous
- Peak-season repairs are expensive and delayed

### Monsoon Prep (July)
- Check condensate drain
- Inspect for debris accumulation
- Ensure proper humidity handling
- Clean outdoor unit after storms

### Post-Monsoon Check (September)
- Clear debris from outdoor unit
- Check for storm damage
- Inspect ductwork for moisture issues
- Prepare for reduced usage

## What Professional Service Includes

### Standard Tune-Up:
- Clean evaporator and condenser coils
- Check refrigerant levels and pressures
- Inspect electrical connections
- Test capacitors and contactors
- Lubricate moving parts
- Check thermostat operation
- Clear condensate drain
- Inspect ductwork connections
- Test system performance
- Document readings and observations

### Time Required:
- Typical tune-up: 45-90 minutes
- More extensive service: 2-3 hours

## DIY vs. Professional Service

### DIY Tasks (Monthly):
- Filter replacement
- Clearing debris from outdoor unit
- Checking thermostat operation
- Visual inspection

### Professional Tasks (Annual):
- Refrigerant check (requires certification)
- Electrical testing
- Coil cleaning (deep clean)
- System performance testing
- Safety control inspection
