# What Does a Professional AC Service Include? A Complete Checklist

A professional AC tune-up is more comprehensive than many homeowners realize. Understanding what's included helps you appreciate the value and know what to expect when a technician services your system.

## Outdoor Unit (Condenser) Service

### Cleaning and Inspection

**Coil Cleaning:**
- Inspect condenser coils for dirt and debris
- Apply appropriate cleaning solution
- Rinse coils thoroughly
- Straighten bent fins if needed

**Component Inspection:**
- Check compressor operation and amperage
- Inspect fan blade for damage or balance
- Examine fan motor condition
- Check refrigerant lines for damage

### Electrical Checks

**Connections and Components:**
- Tighten all electrical connections
- Test capacitor(s) for proper function
- Check contactor condition
- Verify proper voltage and amperage

### System Area

**Exterior Environment:**
- Clear debris from around unit
- Verify adequate clearance
- Check concrete pad condition
- Ensure proper drainage

## Indoor Unit (Air Handler) Service

### Evaporator Coil

**Coil Inspection:**
- Check for dirt and debris
- Inspect for mold or mildew
- Clean if accessible
- Verify proper airflow

### Blower Assembly

**Blower Maintenance:**
- Inspect blower wheel condition
- Check for dirt buildup
- Verify motor operation
- Test amperage draw

### Drain System

**Condensate Management:**
- Clear condensate drain line
- Check drain pan condition
- Verify proper drainage
- Flush with cleaning solution

### Air Filter

**Filter Service:**
- Check current filter condition
- Replace or clean as needed
- Verify correct filter size
- Discuss optimal replacement schedule

## System Controls

### Thermostat

**Thermostat Testing:**
- Verify calibration accuracy
- Check programming function
- Test temperature differential
- Evaluate placement

### Safety Controls

**Safety Verification:**
- Test high-pressure switch
- Check low-pressure switch
- Verify overflow switch operation
- Test all safety circuits

## Refrigerant System

### Refrigerant Check

**Charge Verification:**
- Measure operating pressures
- Calculate superheat and subcooling
- Compare to manufacturer specifications
- Identify potential leaks

### Leak Detection

**If Low Charge Found:**
- Perform leak detection
- Identify leak locations
- Recommend repair options
- Not a normal maintenance item (additional charge applies)

## Performance Testing

### Temperature Differential

**Cooling Capacity:**
- Measure supply air temperature
- Measure return air temperature
- Calculate temperature drop
- Should be 15-20°F across coil

### Airflow Verification

**System Airflow:**
- Check for adequate airflow
- Identify restrictions
- Verify duct connections
- Note any ductwork issues

## Documentation and Reporting

### Service Report

**What You Should Receive:**
- All readings and measurements documented
- Any issues identified
- Recommendations for repairs
- Suggested improvements
- Filter size and type notation

### Before and After

**Good Companies Provide:**
- Current system status
- Work performed
- Conditions found
- Next service recommendations

## What's NOT Included

### Additional Services (Extra Charge)

**Repairs:**
- Parts replacement
- Refrigerant addition
- Electrical repairs
- Component replacement

**Major Services:**
- Duct cleaning
- Deep coil cleaning
- Refrigerant leak repair
- Major electrical work

## Time Required

### Typical Service Duration

- Basic tune-up: 45-60 minutes
- Comprehensive service: 60-90 minutes
- Additional time for issues found

### Factors Affecting Time

- System condition
- Accessibility of components
- Problems discovered
- Equipment age and type

## Frequency Recommendations

### Standard Recommendation

- Annual service minimum
- Spring timing for AC systems
- Before heavy usage begins

### Consider Twice Yearly If:

- System runs year-round
- Older equipment (10+ years)
- Previous problems
- Allergy or health concerns

## Quality Indicators

### Signs of Thorough Service

Look for technicians who:

- Work systematically through checklist
- Take measurements and document
- Explain findings clearly
- Offer honest recommendations
- Don't pressure for unnecessary services

### Red Flags

Be cautious if technician:

- Rushes through service
- Doesn't check both indoor and outdoor units
- Can't show documentation of work
- Pressures for immediate expensive repairs
